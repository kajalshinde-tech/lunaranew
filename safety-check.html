SMALL STEPS. MORE CONFIDENCE.

A little care
goes a long way.

You don’t owe anyone your personal information, your time, or a second date. Trust your instincts and take things at your own pace.

⌘
01 / KEEP IT PERSONAL, NOT PRIVATE

Protect your
little world.

Let someone get to know you without handing over your private details.

  • Keep your home address, workplace details, and passwords private.
  • Never share an OTP, banking information, or identity documents with a match.
  • Be cautious if someone rushes you to another app or sends unexpected links.
↗
02 / MAKE A THOUGHTFUL PLAN

First dates.
Familiar ground.

A simple plan can help you feel more comfortable when meeting someone new.

  • Choose a busy public place and arrange your own transport.
  • Tell someone you trust where you’re going and when you’ll check in.
  • Keep your belongings and drink with you. Leave whenever you want to.
♡
03 / YOUR COMFORT COMES FIRST

A yes is yours.
So is a no.

Respect is the foundation of a good connection, online and in person.

  • Consent should be freely given and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Don’t pressure anyone to share photos, meet up, or move faster.
  • End a conversation if it feels uncomfortable. You don’t need to justify it.

Clear answers for moments when something feels uncertain.

What if a match asks me for money?

Don’t send money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or financial information to someone you’ve met online, even if their story sounds urgent. Stop the conversation and use available report tools or contact support.

What if someone makes me uncomfortable?

You can stop replying, decline a meeting, or leave a date. Use block or report options where available. To raise a concern with Lunara, visit our contact page and share only the details you’re comfortable providing.

Contact Lunara ↗
Does a verified photo guarantee someone is safe?

No. A photo or identity check does not guarantee a person’s intentions or behaviour. Continue to protect your information, meet in public, and trust your judgement.

How should we handle a bill on a date?

Discuss expectations before ordering. Check the menu and any additional charges, pay through a trusted method, and never share a PIN or OTP. You’re free to suggest splitting the bill or choosing a different venue.

Can I change my mind about meeting?

Yes, at any time. Agreeing to chat or meet doesn’t commit you to anything further. Respect the other person’s decision in the same way you’d want yours respected.
