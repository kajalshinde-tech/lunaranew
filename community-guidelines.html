Community Guidelines

LUNARA is a place for connection, fun, and respect. Let's keep it that way.

Respect Everyone

Treat others as you would like to be treated. Harassment, bullying, and hate speech have no place in LUNARA. Be kind, be inclusive, and be respectful of everyone's boundaries.

Be Authentic

LUNARA is built on trust. Use your real name, use your real photos, and be yourself. AI-generated photos, catfishing, and impersonation are strictly prohibited.

Play Fair

Respect the venues and their rules. Show up for your bookings, or cancel in advance. Use the payment split feature honestly and fairly with your group.

Respect Privacy

Don't share other people's private information without their consent. Respect the "Venue Privacy" choices of others and don't record or photograph people without permission.

Zero Tolerance

We have zero tolerance for illegal activities, violence, or sexual harassment. Any violation of these guidelines may lead to immediate account suspension or permanent ban.

Together, We Own the Night.

By using LUNARA, you agree to uphold these values and help us build a safe, fun, and vibrant nightlife community.
