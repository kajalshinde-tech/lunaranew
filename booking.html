Safety First

User-Controlled Plan Privacy System

Posting public plans online can be risky, especially for female users. LUNARA ensures that the user decides what to show, to whom, and when.

The Problem: Fully public plans allow anyone to track and show up uninvited.

The Solution: The "Request to View Plan" logic.

OPTION A

Full Public Visibility

The venue and exact time are visible to everyone. Not recommended for solo outings. ⚠ High Tracking Risk.

OPTION B ★ HIGHLY RECOMMENDED

Limited Public Discovery

Only general Area and Timeframe are shown. Exact venue details remain hidden until you personally approve a match request.

OPTION C

Private / Invite-Only

Ultimate privacy. Your trip is hidden from everyone. You manually browse and send encrypted invites only to users you trust.

Confirmed Meetups Only

"Every meetup on LUNARA is confirmed, tracked, and trusted." Say goodbye to casual flaking.

Trusted Match Badge

Only allow meetups with verified users sporting a high reliability score. Look for the trusted badge before confirming.

Double Confirmation

Before finalizing, both users are hit with a pre-meet chat nudge: "Are you sure you will attend?" Both users must confirm.

Reliability Score

Every user has a visible rating (e.g. 95% Reliable) based on show-ups and authentic behavior. Good behavior is publicly rewarded.
